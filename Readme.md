## Name Derivation

- Sniffle (verb) means to repeatedly sniff audibly through the nose, typically because of a head cold, allergies, or crying.
The sniffles (noun, informal) refers to a condition involving a constant runny or stuffy nose, usually as a result of a minor illness like a common cold.

- Automatic (adjective) means working by itself with little or no direct human control, or done without conscious thought or intention (involuntary/reflexive).
